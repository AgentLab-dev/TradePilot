# Office Politics — Power-Map Worksheet & Playbooks

Reference for `office-politics-navigation`.

## Power-Map Worksheet

Fill this out before any contested decision or initiative. Copy and complete:

```
OBJECTIVE: ____________________________________________
(the one outcome I want)

DECISION OWNER: _______________  | Leaning: For / Neutral / Against
  - What they care most about: ________________________
  - How my goal serves that: __________________________

INFLUENCERS (whose opinion the owner trusts):
  1. ______________  | For / Neutral / Against | Their interest: ______
  2. ______________  | For / Neutral / Against | Their interest: ______

GATEKEEPERS (control access/info/schedule): ____________

BLOCKERS (who loses if I win, and why):
  1. ______________  | What they lose: _________________
     Can I address it? ________________________________

ALLIES (shared interest, even partial):
  1. ______________  | What we share: _________________
  2. ______________  | What I can offer them: __________

NECESSARY support: ____________________________________
SUFFICIENT support: ___________________________________
Who can quietly KILL this: ____________________________

MY MOVE: ______________________________________________
FIRST CONVERSATION (with whom, saying what): ___________
```

## Influence Currencies — Worked Examples

| Currency | A concrete play |
|----------|-----------------|
| Recognition | In a channel their manager reads: "X's fix unblocked the whole release — clutch." |
| Information | "Heads up before the meeting — leadership is focused on audit risk this quarter." |
| Reliability | Hit a small commitment visibly and on time; repeat. Trust compounds. |
| Relief | "I'll take the deployment notes off your plate this cycle." |
| Access | "You two should talk — A owns the data B needs." |

Deposit before you withdraw. Track lightly; never make it transactional out loud.

## Managing Up — Templates

**No-surprises bad news:**
> "Flagging early: [issue] may affect [deliverable]. Here's the plan and two options. Want your call on direction by [date]."

**Make the boss successful:**
> "For your leadership update — here are three wins from our side this sprint, framed for the exec audience."

**Narrate your value (review-proofing):**
> Keep a running "brag doc": date, what shipped, the impact, who it unblocked. Update weekly; pull from it at review time.

## Decoding Corporate-Speak — Extended

| They say | Often means | Your move |
|----------|-------------|-----------|
| "Let's take this offline" | Stop airing this here | Agree, then control the offline framing |
| "Let's pilot it" | No decision; momentum parked | Accept + make your guardrails the pilot's entry criteria |
| "Help me understand…" | I disagree, building a case | Give clean facts; don't get defensive |
| "Per my last message…" | You ignored me; on record now | Respond substantively, on record |
| "We're aligned on the goal" | We disagree on method | Surface the method gap explicitly |
| "Interesting / noted" | No | Don't mistake politeness for a yes |
| "Quick question" | Possibly a trap or a big ask | Clarify scope before answering |
| "With all due respect" | Disrespect incoming | Stay composed; let them overreach |

## Reclaiming Credit — Scripts

- **Hijacked idea:** "Glad it landed — building on what I proposed last week, next step is…"
- **Group work, your contribution:** "The part I drove was [X]; happy to keep owning that thread."
- **Crediting others (builds your capital):** "This worked because [name] caught [thing] early."

## Choosing Battles — Decision Filter

```
1. Does this touch my goals or integrity?      No → let it go.
2. Can I actually change the outcome?           No → document position, move on.
3. Relationship cost vs. prize?                 Prize must clearly exceed cost.
4. Is there a quieter path (private word)?      Usually yes — try it first.
```

Spend political capital like money: rarely, deliberately, on things that matter.

## Self-Protection Checklist

- [ ] Key decisions confirmed in writing ("Confirming we agreed…")
- [ ] No orphan ownership accepted silently
- [ ] Nothing angry ever sent (assume forwardable)
- [ ] Risk that isn't mine handed to its owner, on record
- [ ] A running decision/contribution log kept (accuracy, not ammo)
