## ⭐ "Three Good" backtest — what it taught us (keep referencing)

_50 weekly put-credit trades/name, ~12 mo:_

| Name | Win rate | Avg win | Avg loss | Held-to-expiry | With close-stop |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| NNE | 82% | +$44 | −$106 | **+$849** | +$656 |
| HOOD | 84% | +$36 | −$264 | **−$600** | −$496 |
| MARA | 86% | +$27 | −$115 | +$356 | **+$1,019** |

- **HOOD won 84% and still LOST** — avg loss 7× avg win; thin credit-to-width. **Fix: sell ≥25%-of-width credit, or trade HOOD as a call debit spread.**
- **MARA loves the close-stop** (+$356 → +$1,019) — it trends, so cut losers on the close.
- **NNE whipsaws** — use a $-stop (2× credit) + small size, take 50% fast; a hard close-stop *costs* you.
- **One max loss erases 6–10 wins.** Size for the tail. The **event gate** is the #1 loss-avoider.

## Glossary refresher

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Call debit spread** | Buy a call + sell a higher call. Bullish, defined risk, max loss = debit. Use when IV is **low** (buying is cheap). |
| **Put credit spread** | Sell a put + buy a lower put. Bullish/neutral, defined risk, collect credit up front. Use when IV is **high** (selling is rich). |
| **Max loss** | Debit spread → the debit paid. Credit spread → width − credit. |
| **Max profit** | Debit spread → width − debit. Credit spread → the credit collected. |
| **Breakeven** | Debit: long strike + debit. Credit: short strike − credit. |
| **IV crush** | High IV collapsing → helps the seller (credit), hurts the buyer (debit). |
| **Theta** | Time decay → friend when selling, enemy when buying. |
