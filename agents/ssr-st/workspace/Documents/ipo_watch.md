## The only hard timing signal: the public S-1 → roadshow clock

A **confidential** S-1 does NOT lock a date. The countdown starts when the company files its
**public S-1**, which must land **≥ 15–21 days before the roadshow**. So:

- **No public S-1 yet → no near-term IPO.** Reported "windows" are speculation until then.
- **Public S-1 appears → IPO ≈ 3–5 weeks later.** That's the cue to make sure your IPO Access
  request is in.

**Action now:** request IPO Access early on Robinhood for both names (allocations are tiny and
pro-rated — getting in the queue is the edge). Then watch for the public S-1 filing.

## Playbook when either gets allocated (apply STNOW Step-0 intake)

1. **It's an allocation, not a chase** — at the offer price you're in below the open, day-1 demand for you.
2. **Run STNOW intake**: confirm account (personal), entry basis (allocation), **intent (flip vs hold — ask), exit target, size.**
3. **Default lean = flip into day-1 / early strength** and lock green; the allocation discount + day-1 demand both decay after day one (the SPCX fade). Keep a runner only if it closes strong on heavy volume above the open.
4. **Valuation caution (both):** filing near ~$1T with large GAAP losses + tiny float + index-inclusion mechanics → expect a day-1 pop, then unlock-supply gravity (the SPCX template). Size the flip; don't fall in love.
5. **The SPCX lesson stands:** a confirmed allocation that you plan to flip is a *good* trade — don't let an open-market "AVOID" score talk you out of selling the pop.
